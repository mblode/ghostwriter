# Tone of Voice: [platform]

## Register

Describe demonstrated formality, directness, warmth, and information density. State uncertainty when evidence is sparse or mixed.

## Contexts

Describe only context differences supported by multiple training groups. Name the contexts from the corpus.

## Message shapes

Describe common ordering, paragraph or burst structure, openings, transitions, requests, and closings. Distinguish frequent patterns from occasional ones.

## Language

Record evidenced vocabulary, spelling, punctuation, emoji, capitalization, and formatting patterns. Use frequencies or counts when they are useful. Do not make a universal rule from one example.

## Anti-patterns

List choices the writer demonstrably avoids or corrects. Do not invent dislikes from absence in a small corpus.

## Redacted excerpts

Include three to six varied training excerpts. Each bullet must be at most 280 characters, remove personal identifiers, and use `[redacted]` where removal would make the sentence confusing. Never use held-out text.

## Provenance

Record the training date, platform, record/group/context counts, and evidence limitations. State that split details are recorded in `corpus/manifest.json`. Do not include raw source paths, names, contact details, or held-out content.
